Vegas Vibes: A Blackjack Discord Bot

This Discord Bot was set to me as an exercise to practice my C# abilities. It contains fully interactive blackjack with hit and stand mechanics, dealer logic for automatic gameplay based on standard strategy, and displays card images for both the player and the dealer. There is a virtual balance for users.

This bot is still under development.
